IT & office equipment

Compute, print and office automation for departments, campuses and companies — supplied with licences, warranties and installation documented.

What is included

  • Desktops, all-in-ones, laptops and thin clients
  • Servers, storage and workstations (HPE, HP, Acer)
  • Multifunction and computer printers, scanners, consumables
  • EPABX, telephones and intercom
  • Microsoft Windows and Office, Tally, Busy, Quick Heal and other licences

Typical sites

  • District and directorate offices
  • Computer labs and libraries
  • Corporate branches and plants
  • Research institutes

Security & surveillance

Surveillance, access and life-safety systems specified for the building: camera positions, storage days, door schedules and fire zones are worked out before a single brand is named.

What is included

  • IP and analogue CCTV, NVR/DVR, storage and monitoring
  • Biometric attendance and access control
  • Boom barriers, door-frame and hand-held metal detectors, X-ray baggage scanners
  • Addressable and conventional fire alarm systems
  • Central monitoring and integration with existing systems

Typical sites

  • Secretariats, courts and secure offices
  • Campus perimeters and hostels
  • Hospitals and public buildings
  • Warehouses and plants

Audio-visual systems

Rooms that present: classrooms, boardrooms, auditoriums and control rooms — display, sound and control chosen for the room size, the light and the people who will run it.

What is included

  • Interactive flat panels and boards
  • Projectors and screens
  • LED video walls and controllers
  • Public address and conference audio
  • Room control and cabling

Typical sites

  • Smart classrooms and training centres
  • Boardrooms and conference halls
  • Command and control rooms

Campus networking

Switching, wireless and structured cabling specified against the building plan — port counts, PoE budgets and uplinks that will still be adequate after the next expansion.

What is included

  • Core, distribution and access switches
  • Wireless access points and controllers
  • Structured cabling, racks and passive components
  • Routers, firewalls and internet handover
  • UPS and power protection for network rooms

Typical sites

  • University and college campuses
  • Multi-floor offices
  • Hospitals and secure buildings

Networking in the catalogue

System integration

When a site needs compute, AV, access, cameras and power together, we specify them as one bill of quantity, commission them as one system and hand over one number to call.

What is included

  • Requirement study and site survey
  • Single BOQ with compliance sheets for the tender
  • Delivery, installation, testing and commissioning
  • Operator training and as-built documentation
  • Annual maintenance and support contracts

Typical sites

  • New office buildings and campuses
  • Control rooms and data centres
  • Turnkey refurbishments

Civic & specialised programmes

Equipment for Swachh Bharat and Ayushman Bharat programmes and for municipal services — supplied for the field conditions of the district, not the showroom.

What is included

  • Municipal solid waste and medical waste management equipment
  • Environmental cleaning and anti-smog units
  • Queue management systems
  • Medical equipment for health programmes

Typical sites

  • Municipal corporations and nagar palikas
  • District hospitals and health centres
  • Public utilities

Website development, hosting & management

Institution and company websites built, put on a reliable host with SSL and mail, and kept updated by the same house that supplies your machines.

Cyber security software

Licensed security software for endpoints, networks and mail. Because products in this line change quickly, we specify against the requirement and confirm the current package, licence term and support before quoting.

What is included

  • Endpoint protection and antivirus licensing (site and user counts)
  • Firewall and network security subscriptions
  • Email security and backup
  • Deployment, policy set-up and renewal tracking

Typical sites

  • Offices moving from consumer antivirus to managed protection
  • Campuses and labs with many endpoints
  • Tenders that name compliance standards

Forensic systems

Two lines: forensic laboratory set-up software and forensic workstations. Both are specified against the laboratory — its disciplines, evidence workflow and accreditation needs — and models are confirmed before a bid.

What is included

  • Laboratory set-up and case-management software
  • Forensic analysis workstations with write-blocked storage
  • Peripherals, imaging and secure storage
  • Installation, validation and training

Typical sites

  • State and regional forensic science laboratories
  • Police and investigation units
  • University forensic departments

Drones (unmanned aerial systems)

Specify the site and the task — survey, surveillance, inspection or spraying — and we propose the airframe, payload and training that fit, with current models confirmed against the requirement.

What is included

  • Airframe and payload selection (camera, thermal, mapping)
  • Batteries, chargers and ground equipment
  • Operator training and documentation
  • Support and spares

Typical sites

  • Municipal and utility survey work
  • Campus and perimeter surveillance
  • Agriculture and infrastructure inspection
