    Frequently asked questions

    Are you registered on GeM (Government e-Marketplace)?

    Yes. Shalvi Technologies is a GeM-listed seller. Government and PSU buyers can place the order on GeM; we confirm the live product listing and price against your requirement first.

    Do you publish prices on the website?

    No. Prices depend on the model, quantity, delivery site and whether the purchase is on GeM or by tender. Send the requirement and we reply with a quotation.

    Which areas do you serve?

    We are based in Lucknow and work for government, PSU, campus and corporate sites across Uttar Pradesh.

    Do you support the equipment after installation?

    Yes. Handover includes operator training, and we service the installed base after the invoice.

    Can you supply against a tender specification?

    Yes. We prepare compliance sheets against the specification and confirm the brands and models we quote before the bid.

    Do you also build and host websites?

    Yes. We build institution and company websites, host them with SSL and organisation email, and keep them updated.
